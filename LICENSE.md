## 3. Source attribution

### 3.1 Wikipedia

The majority of the game catalog — 46 platforms, ~62,000 rows — was compiled
from individual "List of X games" articles on the English-language Wikipedia.
Every release row carries a `source_url` column linking back to the article
it was derived from.

**License:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (same as the compiled database)

**Attribution text (include with any distribution):**

> Portions of this database are derived from Wikipedia articles under the
> Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License. Source article URLs
> are preserved in the `source_url` column of each release row.

### 3.1.1 Wikidata (V1.14.3 addition)

`enrich_releases_wikidata.py` populates publisher / developer / genre /
media_type / per-region release dates on the `releases` table by
joining each title to its Wikidata Q-item via the MediaWiki API and
fetching the structured properties via the public SPARQL endpoint
(`https://query.wikidata.org/sparql`).

**License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication) —
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

**Attribution:** Not required by CC0; included here for clarity:

> Game metadata enrichment derived from Wikidata
> (https://www.wikidata.org/), released into the public domain under
> CC0 1.0.

**What we pull:**
- P123 (publisher), P178 (developer), P136 (genre)
- P437 (distribution format) → `media_type`
- P577 (publication date) qualified by P291 (place of publication) for
  Japan (Q17), United States (Q30, our NA proxy), European Union
  (Q458), and Brazil (Q155)

The Wikidata Q-IDs themselves are cached in `release_wikidata_qids`
in `nicks_emporium.db` to make the enrichment script idempotent
across re-runs.

### 3.1.2 Hardware catalog (V1.21.0 addition)

`seed_data/hardware.tsv` — the console / handheld / home-computer /
accessory catalog (118 models across 44 platforms, including 31
peripheral SKUs) — was compiled from the individual hardware articles on
the English-language Wikipedia ("PlayStation 2 models", "Nintendo DS
family", "Xbox 360 § Hardware revisions", etc.). Every row carries a
`source_url` column linking back to the article it was derived from.

The fields themselves (model names, model numbers, regional launch dates,
launch prices) are **facts** and not subject to copyright under *Feist v.
Rural* and equivalents; the compilation is treated as CC BY-SA 4.0 to
match the Wikipedia-sourced game catalog above.

**License:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (same as the compiled database)

**Attribution text (include with any distribution):**

> Hardware-catalog data is derived from Wikipedia articles under the
> Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License. Source article URLs
> are preserved in the `source_url` column of each hardware row.

### 3.2 GeoNames

Two GeoNames datasets are redistributed under `seed_data/`:

- **`postal_codes.tsv`** — Postal-code data for 18 countries (US, GB, DE,
  FR, ES, IT, NL, JP, AU, CA, BR, KR, SE, NO, DK, CH, PL, CZ) from
  GeoNames' per-country postal-code export at
  `https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/`.
- **`city_populations.tsv`** — V1.11 addition; V1.13.4 upgrade. Derived
  from GeoNames' `cities500` dump
  (`https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip`),
  filtered to the same 18 countries and to cities with population ≥ 500.
  Used by the simulator to weight per-city address sampling by population.
  Pre-V1.13.4 used the smaller `cities1000` dump (≥1,000); the upgrade
  adds ~35% more long-tail towns and villages for better small-place
  realism. Build script: `build_seed_city_populations.py`.

**License:** CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

**Modifications declared (CC BY 4.0 §3(b)):** the cities500 dump (and,
pre-V1.13.4, the cities1000 dump) was filtered (country subset +
minimum-population threshold) and projected to 8 columns (country_code,
name, ascii_name, admin_code1, admin_code2, population, latitude,
longitude); no row values were altered.

**Attribution text:**

> Geographic data (postal codes and city populations) from GeoNames.org
> (https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

### 3.3 OpenStreetMap — street names

`seed_data/city_streets.tsv` is derived from OpenStreetMap data
(per-country PBF extracts from `https://download.geofabrik.de/`),
filtered to highway-tagged ways with a `name` tag, spatially joined
to the top-50 cities per country, and aggregated to the top ~100
streets per city by OSM frequency. Used by the simulator to generate
real, locally-appropriate street names (Deansgate in Manchester,
Old Kent Road in London, Champs-Élysées in Paris, …) instead of the
small editorial generic list used pre-V1.12.

`seed_data/generic_streets.tsv` (added V1.13.1) is a second-order
derivative: streets that appear in many distinct cities within a
country, mined from `city_streets.tsv`. Top 30 per country. Used as
a fallback street bank for customers in long-tail towns outside the
top-50 OSM-covered set. Same provenance, same license.

**License:** Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 —
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

**Modifications declared (ODbL §4.5):** filtered to 18 countries ×
top-50 cities × top-~100 streets by OSM frequency; projected to 4
columns (country_code, city_name, street_name, osm_count); no
geometry or tag values are reproduced.

**Attribution text (include with any distribution):**

> Street-name data derived from OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap
> contributors. Available under the Open Database License:
> https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

**Share-alike requirement:** the derived `seed_data/city_streets.tsv`

### 3.4 Local-archive filename parsing (Commodore Amiga, Commodore 64)

`amiga_games.csv` and `c64_games.csv` were produced by parsing filenames from
locally-held TOSEC-named preservation archives (not scraped from Wikipedia).

**Scope of what this project includes and excludes:**

- **Included:** filename-derived metadata only — title, year, publisher,
  media type, regional indicators, and hardware compatibility flags.
- **Not included:** no disk images, ROM files, or copyrighted software from
  these archives is distributed with this project. Copyright on the games
  themselves remains with their original publishers.
- **Legal basis for filename metadata:** titles, dates, and publisher names
  are facts and not subject to copyright under *Feist v. Rural* (US) and
  equivalent doctrines in other jurisdictions.
- **TOSEC:** the naming convention used by the source archives is a
  community-documented preservation standard.

### 3.5 Sources deliberately NOT used

The following third-party sources were deliberately excluded to keep the
distribution license clean:

- **Hall of Light (abime.net)** — Amiga database, copyrighted by HOL Team
  with separate database-compilation rights asserted. Used for
  cross-reference only; not scraped or ingested.
- **MobyGames, IGDB, GiantBomb, GameFAQs** — commercial databases whose
  Terms of Service prohibit bulk extraction and redistribution.
- **Royal Mail Postcode Address File (PAF)** — proprietary and paid.
- **Canada Post full 6-character postcodes** — proprietary and paid. Only
  FSA-level data (first three characters, publicly licensed) is included.
- **Metacritic, OpenCritic** — review-aggregator data outside project scope.
